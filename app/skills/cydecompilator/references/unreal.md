# Unreal game reconstruction knowledge

Discover and describe `unreal_rules` and `unreal_guide`. They expose the portable knowledge catalog from the Halo Anniversary → Unreal 5.8 prototype, with general methodology, title-specific rules and ten campaign profiles. They do not execute or import a game.

Call with `profile=generic` for methodology, or `profile=halo-cea-x360` for the recorded prototype. Use a returned category id to filter and `language=en|fr`. `unreal_guide` returns the same rules as Markdown. Both are read-only and independent of an open project or the research module.

Bind actual work to the source executable/edition and assets. Follow the returned geometry, UV, animation, collision, weapons, AI, vehicle, effect and evidence requirements. Original files remain read-only; write generated projects separately. Anniversary visuals and native C++ Unreal gameplay are constraints of this profile. Do not apply its decoders or exact-image assumptions to arbitrary games.

Catalog reference hashes identify a documentation/source snapshot. They are not a validation receipt. Reading the guide or compiling a generated project cannot satisfy behavior comparison. Retain source/artifact hashes, scenario assertions, captures, unknown behavior and fallbacks. Full commercial-game equivalence is not certified.
